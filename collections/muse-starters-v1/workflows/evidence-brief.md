# A Source-Grounded Answer

Investigate a bounded question and audit the resulting claims.

1. **Turn a Question Into an Evidence Plan** — load `skills/question-to-evidence/SKILL.md` for this step, carry forward the previous step's real output, and stop before any unapproved external effect.
2. **Audit a Claim Against Its Actual Evidence** — load `skills/claim-audit/SKILL.md` for this step, carry forward the previous step's real output, and stop before any unapproved external effect.

## Finish
Return a supported conclusion, exact evidence locations and the remaining uncertainty.

A workflow is a sequence for Muse to follow, not a background service or dispatch permission.
