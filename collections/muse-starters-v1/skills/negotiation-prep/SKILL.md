# Prepare a Negotiation Around Facts and Alternatives

## Use when
A person needs to prepare for a work, project or offer discussion.

## Not for
Inventing experience, credentials, endorsements or interview outcomes, or sending applications without permission.

## Inputs
- The actual offer or proposed terms
- The person’s priorities, alternatives and constraints

## Procedure
1. List the terms and identify missing or ambiguous conditions.
2. Rank the person’s priorities rather than assuming pay is the only concern.
3. Separate a preferred outcome from a genuine walk-away boundary.
4. Draft questions and tradeoffs grounded in supported facts.
5. Produce a concise discussion plan without guaranteeing acceptance or giving legal conclusions.

## Deliver
- A priority and tradeoff sheet
- A realistic conversation script

## Check the result
- Invented competing offers are excluded.
- Costs and nonmonetary terms are considered.
- The plan leaves room for the other party’s response.

## Recover or narrow
Retain the person’s actual record and voice. Mark an unsupported requirement as a gap and strengthen the supported evidence instead of manufacturing credentials or guarantees.

## Example request
Help me compare these two project offers and prepare a conversation about scope, payment timing and support obligations.

## Design refinement
Adds explicit alternatives and total-term comparison rather than aggressive scripted demands.
