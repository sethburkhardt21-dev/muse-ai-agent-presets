# Turn Customer Assumptions Into Testable Questions

## Use when
A product or service idea needs customer evidence before investment.

## Not for
Fabricated customer evidence, deceptive outreach, unapproved spending, or treating proposed commitments as accepted.

## Inputs
- The proposed customer and problem
- Existing observations and constraints on outreach

## Procedure
1. Separate known customer behavior from the builder’s assumptions.
2. Identify the riskiest assumption that a conversation or observation can test.
3. Draft neutral questions about recent behavior rather than leading praise-seeking questions.
4. Define what evidence would change the offer or stop the idea.
5. Return an interview guide and evidence log; send no outreach unless asked through an authorized tool.

## Deliver
- A nonleading interview guide
- A decision rule and evidence log

## Check the result
- Questions do not assume the proposed solution is desirable.
- Hypothetical enthusiasm is not called purchase evidence.
- Outreach remains a draft unless actually authorized and sent.

## Recover or narrow
Separate confirmed evidence from assumptions. Produce a reversible next experiment or draft, and do not turn a missing integration into a reason to abandon useful analysis.

## Example request
Help me test whether small clinics need this document workflow before I build it.

## Design refinement
Adds disconfirmation criteria and behavioral questions to customer-discovery scripts.
