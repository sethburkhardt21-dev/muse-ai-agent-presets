# Map a Lecture Before Deep Study

## Use when
The learner wants a conceptual overview of slides or notes before detailed study.

## Not for
Inventing source facts, supplying answers to a live restricted assessment, or claiming clinical credentialing.

## Inputs
- The actual lecture material
- The exam or learning objective and any source-only constraint

## Procedure
1. Locate the actual lecture boundaries and read the relevant contiguous material, including diagrams that carry meaning.
2. Extract a bounded set of concepts and retain the source label and location for each.
3. Arrange prerequisite and mechanism links, explicitly separating source statements from instructor-style organizing inferences.
4. Identify easily confused pairs and exceptions. Carry units, direction and threshold conditions with every important number.
5. Select a reading sequence that resolves prerequisites first; give each section a question it should answer.
6. Check the map against the source and keep missing or unreadable figures unresolved rather than filling them from memory.

## Deliver
- A concept-first lecture outline
- A short self-check and source-indexed fact list
- Deliver a small concept hierarchy, directional prerequisite edges, slide/page anchors, key contrasts, numeric facts with conditions, and a short reading route. Label inferred teaching links separately from explicit lecture claims. Preserve the original lecture file.

## Check the result
- No outside facts are added when the task is source-only.
- Numbers preserve their units, context and exceptions.
- The overview does not replace the distinctions the exam material emphasizes.
- The task-specific structured output is checked against source facts or disclosed adjudication, while semantic usefulness still requires human review.

## Recover or narrow
Keep source revision, page anchors, concept IDs, explicit versus inferred edge labels and unreadable regions. Re-map only changed lecture sections and never silently merge different course versions.

## Example request
Give me a concept map of this kidney-failure lecture before I study it; use only the uploaded slides.

## Design refinement
The reviewed entrypoint is a broad study planner, not a detailed source-location and concept-edge compiler; this skill adds that narrower capability without claiming superior learning outcomes.
