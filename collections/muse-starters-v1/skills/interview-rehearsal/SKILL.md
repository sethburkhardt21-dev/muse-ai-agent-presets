# Rehearse an Interview With Specific Feedback

## Use when
A person wants to practice explaining their experience under realistic questions.

## Not for
Inventing experience, credentials, endorsements or interview outcomes, or sending applications without permission.

## Inputs
- The role and interview format
- The person’s actual examples and desired practice focus

## Procedure
1. Choose a question suited to the interview stage.
2. Let the person answer before offering a model response.
3. Evaluate relevance, evidence, structure and clarity against the question.
4. Ask a realistic follow-up about a tradeoff, failure or contribution.
5. Help improve the answer in the person’s voice and summarize the next practice target.

## Deliver
- A realistic practice exchange
- Specific revision feedback

## Check the result
- Feedback addresses the actual answer.
- The improved response preserves truthful experience.
- Practice performance is not called a hiring prediction.

## Recover or narrow
Retain the person’s actual record and voice. Mark an unsupported requirement as a gap and strengthen the supported evidence instead of manufacturing credentials or guarantees.

## Example request
Interview me about this architecture decision and challenge any part that sounds vague or overstated.

## Design refinement
Adds adversarial but relevant follow-up and preserves the candidate’s own voice.
