# Design a Small Practice Session With Feedback

## Use when
A person wants steady progress in a hobby without an unrealistic program.

## Not for
Making purchases or bookings without approval, guessing current restrictions, or treating uncertain conditions as safe.

## Inputs
- The hobby, current level and specific difficulty
- Available equipment, time and preferred style of practice

## Procedure
1. Choose one skill small enough to practice in the available session.
2. Define a concrete exercise with an observable result.
3. Adjust difficulty to the person’s current level and resources.
4. Use their actual attempt to give targeted feedback.
5. Suggest the next practice variation based on the observed result, not a fixed streak demand.

## Deliver
- A manageable practice session
- A feedback and progression note

## Check the result
- The exercise targets the stated difficulty.
- Progress is judged from actual attempts.
- The plan does not create unnecessary purchases or unsafe physical demands.

## Recover or narrow
Keep the plan useful with explicit contingencies. Recheck time-sensitive constraints before a commitment and leave purchases, reservations and physical actions to the authorized route.

## Example request
Give me a 15-minute drawing exercise for perspective, then review my attempt against one clear objective.

## Design refinement
Adds feedback-driven progression and equipment awareness to generic habit advice.
