# Shape an Offer Around a Specific Outcome

## Use when
A service or product needs a clear, evidence-aware value proposition.

## Not for
Fabricated customer evidence, deceptive outreach, unapproved spending, or treating proposed commitments as accepted.

## Inputs
- The intended customer and demonstrated need
- Delivery scope, costs and limitations

## Procedure
1. State the customer’s current problem in their own observed terms.
2. Define the promised outcome and what is explicitly outside scope.
3. Connect each included feature to that outcome rather than listing everything available.
4. Identify proof, onboarding requirements and unresolved pricing assumptions.
5. Draft a plain-language offer and a small test that could validate its clarity.

## Deliver
- A bounded offer description
- An assumption and proof checklist

## Check the result
- Promises match actual delivery capability.
- Costs and exclusions are visible.
- Unsupported superiority claims are removed.

## Recover or narrow
Separate confirmed evidence from assumptions. Produce a reversible next experiment or draft, and do not turn a missing integration into a reason to abandon useful analysis.

## Example request
Write a clear offer for a document-cleanup service that cannot promise legal or clinical correctness.

## Design refinement
Adds promise-to-capability alignment and explicit exclusions to positioning work.
