# Plan an Exam Preparation Cycle

## Use when
The learner needs a study plan that fits the exam scope and available time.

## Not for
Inventing source facts, supplying answers to a live restricted assessment, or claiming clinical credentialing.

## Inputs
- The exam date, source scope and available study blocks
- Current strengths, missed questions and required topics

## Procedure
1. Recover the exam date, allowed materials, topic scope and actual available study windows from supplied records before asking again.
2. Map each topic to its required sources and known prerequisites. Distinguish excluded content from content not yet located.
3. Use recent independent attempts to identify weak objectives. Do not assign a weakness score from an unanswered item or a coached retry.
4. Reserve time for feedback, breaks and mixed practice before allocating the remaining study minutes; disclose any chosen reserve.
5. Allocate whole sessions across priorities within capacity. Include a concrete retrieval target and review criterion in every session.
6. At each checkpoint, recompute from remaining time and observed attempts. Preserve completed work and explicitly reduce optional scope when a day is missed.

## Deliver
- A realistic study sequence
- A revision rule based on observed performance
- Return a topic-by-source coverage matrix, finite session budget, explicitly allocated study and practice blocks, deferred scope, checkpoints and a rescheduling rule. Show arithmetic in minutes and label diagnostic weights as planning heuristics, not validated predictions of exam performance.

## Check the result
- All required topics are represented.
- The schedule fits the available blocks without fabricated free time.
- Weak areas receive practice rather than only more summaries.
- The task-specific structured output is checked against source facts or disclosed adjudication, while semantic usefulness still requires human review.

## Recover or narrow
Preserve the blueprint version, source map, independent attempt IDs, completed blocks, reserve choices and remaining windows. Resume from the next unfinished prerequisite; never infer a reminder or calendar write from the plan itself.

## Example request
I have nine study sessions before the exam and these five slide decks. Build a plan using my missed-question list.

## Design refinement
Add exam-blueprint fidelity, explicit minute accounting and assistance-aware rescheduling rather than a fixed daily-duration default.
