# Organize Family Memories With Provenance and Consent

## Use when
A person wants to organize stories, photographs or family records.

## Not for
Diagnosing conditions, changing treatment, impersonating a professional, or assuming access to another person’s private records.

## Inputs
- The supplied materials and intended audience
- Known identities, dates and sharing permissions

## Procedure
1. Inventory the materials without guessing identities or missing dates.
2. Preserve original files and record captions as supplied or uncertain.
3. Group items into a useful chronology or theme while retaining source links.
4. Draft narrative transitions without inventing family events.
5. Deliver an index or story collection and keep private details out of public copies unless authorized.

## Deliver
- A source-linked memory index
- A clearly grounded narrative draft

## Check the result
- Unknown people and dates remain unknown.
- Originals are preserved.
- Sharing boundaries are respected for living people and sensitive records.

## Recover or narrow
Keep a useful organizational or writing result without presenting it as medical, legal or therapeutic authority. For urgent safety concerns, direct the user to appropriate real-world help.

## Example request
Organize these photos and notes into a family album outline, marking unknown dates rather than guessing.

## Design refinement
Adds uncertainty and privacy handling to sentimental narrative generation.
