# Plan an Event Around Guests and Constraints

## Use when
A gathering needs an organized but proportionate plan.

## Not for
Making purchases or bookings without approval, guessing current restrictions, or treating uncertain conditions as safe.

## Inputs
- Guest count, purpose and date
- Venue, budget, accessibility and food constraints

## Procedure
1. Clarify the event’s main experience and nonnegotiable needs.
2. Build a practical timeline including preparation and cleanup.
3. Assign tasks only to people who have actually agreed to them.
4. Check dependencies such as venue access, food preparation and equipment.
5. Deliver a checklist, contingency plan and drafts for any messages rather than sending automatically.

## Deliver
- An event timeline and checklist
- A guest-needs and contingency plan

## Check the result
- Tasks fit the available people and time.
- Accessibility and dietary needs are not forgotten.
- Proposed responsibilities are not reported as accepted commitments.

## Recover or narrow
Keep the plan useful with explicit contingencies. Recheck time-sensitive constraints before a commitment and leave purchases, reservations and physical actions to the authorized route.

## Example request
Organize a small game night for eight people with simple food and a plan that works if two guests arrive late.

## Design refinement
Adds explicit guest constraints and dependency handling without turning a gathering into enterprise project management.
