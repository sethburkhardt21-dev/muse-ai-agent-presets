# Build a Storyboard With Clear Shot Purpose

## Use when
A video, animation or sequential visual needs a coherent shot plan.

## Not for
Claiming a plan is rendered media, ignoring reference constraints, or publishing assets without appropriate rights.

## Inputs
- The brief and narrative beats
- Target duration, aspect ratio and available assets

## Procedure
1. Recover the requested story, duration, format, assets and already settled creative decisions.
2. Identify the indispensable narrative beats and the visual information each shot must communicate.
3. Create a shot sequence with stable IDs, first-frame occupancy, observable action and continuity state at the cut.
4. Allocate time in frames or seconds using one stated frame rate; include transitions in the duration calculation.
5. Check spatial axis, gaze, prop handoff, entry/exit and action feasibility across neighboring shots.
6. Deliver the complete storyboard, flag any intentionally unresolved production question and specify the smallest test shot before costly generation.

## Deliver
- A timed shot plan
- Asset and continuity requirements
- Deliver stable shot IDs, durations, action beats, camera/composition, dialogue or sound where requested, continuity inputs/outputs and transition intent. The timeline must fit the requested duration and preserve required story beats. A storyboard is not a rendered video.

## Check the result
- Shot durations fit the requested runtime.
- Every shot advances the intended story or information.
- A textual board is not described as a rendered film.
- The task-specific structured output is checked against source facts or disclosed adjudication, while semantic usefulness still requires human review.

## Recover or narrow
Preserve the shot IDs, story beat map, frame rate, durations, overlap rules, geography and continuity state. Resume the unverified shot or transition without redesigning accepted assets or claiming unrendered footage.

## Example request
Plan a 20-second four-shot sequence showing a character discovering a hidden room.

## Design refinement
Add exact frame-budget and transition accounting in a compact storyboard procedure; retain the upstream as a broader film-production benchmark.
