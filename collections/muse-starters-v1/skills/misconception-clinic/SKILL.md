# Repair a Specific Misunderstanding

## Use when
The learner can state an answer but is confused about why it is true or how related concepts differ.

## Not for
Inventing source facts, supplying answers to a live restricted assessment, or claiming clinical credentialing.

## Inputs
- The learner’s explanation or mistaken example
- The relevant source and a concrete learning target

## Procedure
1. Ask for or recover the exact answer and reasoning, using the already supplied source and learning objective.
2. State the smallest suspected wrong rule and at least one alternative explanation such as arithmetic or terminology.
3. Choose a counterexample that separates those explanations while changing only one relevant condition.
4. Explain the corrected rule with its limits and contrast it with the learner's original reasoning.
5. Ask a fresh transfer question without embedding its answer; keep the response separate from the coached example.
6. Close with what changed, what remains untested and a source-linked next practice target.

## Deliver
- A targeted correction with contrast
- A transfer check and concise takeaway
- Deliver the learner's proposed rule, the source-supported correction, a minimal contrast or counterexample, one diagnostic response and a separate transfer check. Record understanding as untested when no fresh response exists.

## Check the result
- The explanation addresses the learner’s actual error.
- Analogies do not contradict the underlying mechanism.
- A correct repeated phrase is not treated as evidence of understanding by itself.
- The task-specific structured output is checked against source facts or disclosed adjudication, while semantic usefulness still requires human review.

## Recover or narrow
Keep the original reasoning, diagnosis alternatives, counterexample, assistance given, source conditions and unanswered transfer item. Do not restart an explanation already understood or silently reclassify a coached response as independent.

## Example request
I keep mixing up clearance and filtration. Use my lecture to show the difference, then test me with one example.

## Design refinement
Add explicit discrimination between conceptual and execution errors, with a separate unseen-transfer status.
