# Turn an Idea Into a Production-Ready Brief — operational depth

Read only the branch needed for the current task. These are working methods, not a demand for additional approvals on ordinary reading or drafting.

## Domain method

A production brief translates intent into observable choices while preserving the authority of each decision. Separate user-confirmed constraints, supplied facts, creative proposals and unknowns. Describe hierarchy, composition, pacing, palette roles and audience response in terms that another creator can act on. Do not use adjectives such as premium or playful as substitutes for visual or narrative decisions.

## Output contract

Deliver a self-contained brief naming the audience, intended response, format, exact protected text/assets, confirmed constraints, proposed execution choices, acceptance checks and material open questions. A proposed direction is not user approval, and a brief is not a rendered artifact.

## Choose the right action

### Two supplied constraints conflict.
Expose the specific conflict and offer the smallest viable alternative while preserving both originals in the record.

**Avoid:** Do not silently discard a mandatory requirement.

### The user asks for a finished artifact, not a brief.
Use the brief as an internal production record and continue to the available rendering or implementation tool.

**Avoid:** Do not present planning as fulfillment of a requested image or video.

### A reference shows a style but its rights or identity cannot be reused.
Extract general visual principles and use original assets or authorized references.

**Avoid:** Do not assume a visible image grants redistribution or identity rights.

### The requested tone is broad.
Propose specific visual or narrative choices tied to the audience and mark them as proposals.

**Avoid:** Do not fabricate audience research or brand history.

### The brief is settled and only a format adaptation is requested.
Preserve positioning and protected assets while adapting layout and technical constraints.

**Avoid:** Do not reopen the entire strategy or replace the approved message.

## Worked example (authored, synthetic)

**Input:** The brief requires a1080 × 1920 poster, the exact line "Open Saturday", and no photographs. The proposed direction is geometric shapes with a large date area.

**Method:** Keep the size, exact copy and image exclusion confirmed; mark the geometric direction proposed.

**Result:** Deliver a vertical vector-style layout brief with "Open Saturday" unchanged, no photo assets, a clear primary message and a legibility check. Approval of the visual direction remains pending.

## Edge or recovery example 1 (authored, synthetic)

**Input:** A follow-up asks for a square adaptation but still includes a mandatory vertical-only template constraint.

**Method:** Identify the format conflict rather than pretending both can be satisfied in one canvas.

**Result:** Return a proposed square adaptation separately and ask which governing format applies before claiming conformance; do not change the protected line.

## Failure diagnosis

### A production team cannot act on the brief.
**Likely cause:** It contains mood adjectives but no concrete choices or deliverables.

**Repair:** Translate each important adjective into visible hierarchy, spacing, imagery or pacing and name the output.

**Recheck:** Another creator can produce a candidate without inventing the core requirements.

### A generated concept changes the supplied tagline.
**Likely cause:** Protected content was treated as editable copy.

**Repair:** Restore exact wording and add a byte-level text check.

**Recheck:** Every protected string matches the governing brief.

### The handoff marks an unchosen direction approved.
**Likely cause:** Internal preference was promoted to user authority.

**Repair:** Change the status to proposed and preserve the alternatives.

**Recheck:** Only an actual recorded user decision establishes acceptance.

## Preserve for the next turn

Preserve confirmed decisions with their sources, proposed alternatives, exact protected material, asset rights and the requested deliverable. Resume the specific production step or unresolved conflict rather than another broad branding interview.
