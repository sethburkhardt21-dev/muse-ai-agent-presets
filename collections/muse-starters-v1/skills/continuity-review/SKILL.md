# Review Continuity Across a Creative Sequence

## Use when
A set of frames, shots or assets needs consistency review.

## Not for
Claiming a plan is rendered media, ignoring reference constraints, or publishing assets without appropriate rights.

## Inputs
- The actual sequence and approved references
- Known intentional changes across scenes

## Procedure
1. Create a small continuity inventory of persistent subject and scene attributes.
2. Compare adjacent shots for appearance, position, direction and state.
3. Separate deliberate storytelling changes from accidental drift.
4. Prioritize fixes by viewer-visible impact and dependency.
5. Recheck corrected frames while preserving already approved material.

## Deliver
- A frame- or shot-specific continuity report
- A narrow revision order

## Check the result
- Findings identify the actual frame or transition.
- Intentional changes are not marked defects.
- Unseen frames are not assumed correct.

## Recover or narrow
Preserve the approved version and reference assets. Revise only the failing dimension, state unrendered portions, and offer a usable partial deliverable instead of claiming a finished production.
