# Assess a Job Against the Person’s Actual Evidence

## Use when
A person is considering a job and wants a grounded fit analysis.

## Not for
Inventing experience, credentials, endorsements or interview outcomes, or sending applications without permission.

## Inputs
- The current job description
- The person’s actual projects, skills and constraints

## Procedure
1. Separate mandatory qualifications from preferred experience and ambiguous wording.
2. Map each important requirement to actual evidence or a named gap.
3. Check location, work arrangement and compensation information without guessing.
4. Identify the strongest credible application angle and a small gap-closing action.
5. Return a fit assessment without pretending to know the employer’s hiring decision.

## Deliver
- A requirement-to-evidence matrix
- A supported application angle

## Check the result
- Unsupported experience is not invented.
- Current vacancy and terms are checked when possible.
- Fit is not presented as guaranteed hiring probability.

## Recover or narrow
Retain the person’s actual record and voice. Mark an unsupported requirement as a gap and strengthen the supported evidence instead of manufacturing credentials or guarantees.

## Example request
Compare this backend role with my three projects and identify the strongest evidence and the real gaps.

## Design refinement
Adds explicit evidence mapping instead of vague encouragement or degree-based dismissal.
