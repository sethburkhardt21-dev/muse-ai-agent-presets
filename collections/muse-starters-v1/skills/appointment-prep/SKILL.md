# Prepare a Clear Appointment Summary

## Use when
A person wants to organize questions and records for a professional appointment.

## Not for
Diagnosing conditions, changing treatment, impersonating a professional, or assuming access to another person’s private records.

## Inputs
- The person’s own notes and appointment purpose
- Existing professional instructions and questions

## Procedure
1. Arrange the reported timeline and concerns without diagnosing them.
2. Separate observations from interpretations and preserve exact supplied instructions.
3. Identify the questions most important to the person.
4. Create a concise summary suitable to share with the professional.
5. Check that no invented medication, symptom or treatment recommendation has entered the summary.

## Deliver
- A concise appointment summary
- A prioritized question list

## Check the result
- The timeline matches the supplied record.
- Professional instructions are not changed.
- Urgent concerns are not handled as routine scheduling problems.

## Recover or narrow
Keep a useful organizational or writing result without presenting it as medical, legal or therapeutic authority. For urgent safety concerns, direct the user to appropriate real-world help.

## Example request
Organize these notes into a one-page appointment summary and questions; do not try to diagnose the problem.

## Design refinement
Adds timeline fidelity and question prioritization while staying organizational rather than clinical.
